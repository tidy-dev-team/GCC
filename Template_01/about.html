About Amel

We are redefining how the construction industry manages its workforce. Our platform serves as a digital labor exchange, enabling construction companies to seamlessly borrow and lend skilled workers based on project needs.

Founded with a vision to empower both businesses and labors, AMEL bridges the gap between supply and demand, ensuring that projects move forward without delays while workers gain continuous employment opportunities.

Our Mission

To create a trusted, transparent, and efficient platform that supports construction companies in optimizing their workforce and empowers labors with secure, fair, and continuous employment.

Our Values

Innovation

We harness technology to simplify complex workforce challenges.

Integrity

We operate with complete transparency and compliance with UAE labor regulations.

Collaboration

We bring together companies, workers, and regulatorson one trusted platform.

Impact

We strive to make a meaningful difference in the lives of workers and the growth of companies.

What we do

Workforce Optimization

Helping companies manage fluctuating manpower needs efficiently.

Regulatory Compliance

Built in partnership with MOHRE and other authorities to ensure every transaction is fully compliant with UAE labor laws.

Seamless Transactions

Integrated with RAKEZ, DED, RAKBANK, and UAE Pass for smooth onboarding, contracts, and financial operations.

Support Team

Dedicated in-house teams providing multilingual support in English & Arabic.

Why Choose AMEL?

With AMEL, companies save time, reduce costs, and maintain compliance while workers benefit from stability, fair treatment, and greater opportunities. Together, we are building a stronger, more connected construction ecosystem for the future.
