About Amel

We are redefining how the construction industry manages its workforce. Our platform serves as a digital labor exchange, enabling construction companies to seamlessly borrow and lend skilled workers based on project needs.

Founded with a vision to empower both businesses and labors, AMEL bridges the gap between supply and demand, ensuring that projects move forward without delays while workers gain continuous employment opportunities.

Our Mission

To create a trusted, transparent, and efficient platform that supports construction companies in optimizing their workforce and empowers labors with secure, fair, and continuous employment.
