Mosadad is the UAE’s first AI-native claims recovery platform, purpose-built for insurers, regulators, and government entities.

Founded by insurance and technology leaders with decades of experience across the Middle East and Africa, we are on a mission to eliminate inefficiencies in motor claim settlements.

Our Vision

A future where every motor claim in the region is settled instantly, with zero disputes, zero paperwork, and full traceability — delivering fairness for insurers, regulators, and customers alike.

Our Mission

To transform claims recovery into a real-time, transparent, and regulator-aligned process that builds trust across the insurance ecosystem and improves road safety outcomes.
